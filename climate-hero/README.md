# Rootwork landing header

A standalone landing-page header for a climate non-profit (Rootwork is a placeholder name). Open `index.html` from any static server.

- On first visit an ASCII cosmos grows from the bottom edge to 60% of the screen height and its petals open. It is drawn on a canvas from the same image as the photo, so the two line up exactly. The replay link in the caption runs it again.
- Hovering shows the real flower and sky through a soft mask that follows the cursor. If the cursor stays, the mask grows to fill the screen; moving away closes it. On touch screens or with the keyboard, the "See the real one" button does the same.
- The headline cuts close up when the real flower is showing.
- With reduced motion turned on, the flower is drawn already grown and the sparkle effect is off.

## Assets

- `assets/flower.webp`: cut out from [a cosmos photo on Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/tender-cosmos-bipinnatus-flower-with-thin-stem-against-blue-sky-5743979/) (Pexels license).
- `assets/sky.webp`: a gradient sky with clouds taken from [a sky photo on Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/blue-sky-and-white-clouds-231009/) (Pexels license).

The CO₂ figures are the Mauna Loa annual means for 1958 and 2024.
