# Rootwork landing header

A standalone landing-page header for a climate non-profit (Rootwork is a placeholder name). Open `index.html` from any static server.

- On load, only the flower is on screen. An ASCII cosmos grows up from the bottom edge as a small green bud on a lengthening stem, reaches 60% of the screen height and opens. Then the nav, headline and copy fade in. Hover does nothing until the flower has grown. The replay link in the caption runs the growth again.
- Once grown, the flower head turns slowly from side to side on its stem (about ±18°). The ASCII and the photo turn together. A single photo has no back, so a full 360° spin would need a 3D model or turntable footage.
- Hovering shows a soft, uneven spotlight that follows the cursor and reveals the real flower and a cloudy sky. A blurred, dimmed ring around it softens the edge into the dark background.
- The headline is set in DotGothic16, a pixel face. Inside the spotlight its letters turn into Geist Mono, a smooth monospaced sans, letter for letter. The rest of the headline stays pixelated.
- The "See the real one" button, or a tap on touch screens, shows the whole photo and sky, with the headline fully smooth. Pressing it again goes back to the render.
- With reduced motion turned on, the flower is drawn already grown, holds still, and everything shows straight away.

## Assets

- `assets/flower.webp`: cut out from [a cosmos photo on Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/tender-cosmos-bipinnatus-flower-with-thin-stem-against-blue-sky-5743979/) (Pexels license).
- `assets/sky.webp`: a gradient sky with a scattering of clouds taken from [a sky photo on Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/blue-sky-and-white-clouds-231009/) (Pexels license).

The CO₂ figures are Mauna Loa readings: about 315 ppm when measurements began in 1958, and the 2024 annual mean of 424.6 ppm.
