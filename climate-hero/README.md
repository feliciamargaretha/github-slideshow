# Rootwork landing header

A standalone landing-page header for a climate non-profit (Rootwork is a placeholder name). Open `index.html` from any static server.

- On load, an ASCII cosmos grows up from the bottom edge as a small green bud on a lengthening stem. It reaches 60% of the screen height and then the petals open. It is drawn on a canvas from the same image as the photo, so the two line up exactly. The replay link in the caption runs it again.
- Hovering shows a spotlight that follows the cursor and reveals the real flower and sky underneath. Moving away closes it.
- The "See the real one" button, or a tap on touch screens, shows the whole photo and sky. Pressing it again goes back to the render.
- The headline is set in Jacquarda Bastarda 9, a pixel calligraphy. Hovering it, or showing the full photo, wipes it across to Instrument Serif.
- With reduced motion turned on, the flower is drawn already grown and the sparkle effect is off.

## Assets

- `assets/flower.webp`: cut out from [a cosmos photo on Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/tender-cosmos-bipinnatus-flower-with-thin-stem-against-blue-sky-5743979/) (Pexels license).
- `assets/sky.webp`: a gradient sky with clouds taken from [a sky photo on Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/blue-sky-and-white-clouds-231009/) (Pexels license).

The CO₂ figures are Mauna Loa readings: about 315 ppm when measurements began in 1958, and the 2024 annual mean of 424.6 ppm.
