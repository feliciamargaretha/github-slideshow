Hi, Tampere
Designers

Glad you found us. We host meetups, critiques and good company for anyone who designs anything.

Come say hi

We're a volunteer-run community for anyone who designs in and around Tampere: interfaces and services, type and posters, objects, games and everything in between.

Students, seniors, career switchers and the design-curious all sit at the same table. Once a month we meet for short talks and show-and-tell, and in between there are crit clubs, hands-on workshops and visits to studios around the city. Come to one, come to all, or just come and listen.

Ways to join in

Four ways to meet other designers in Tampere.

  • Monthly meetups

    Short talks, show-and-tell and snacks, once a month.

  • Crit club

    Bring work in progress and get kind, useful feedback.

  • Workshops

    Hands-on sessions on tools, methods and craft.

  • Studio visits

    A look inside design studios and teams around Tampere.

Questions, answered

Something else on your mind? Ask us at the next meetup or send us a message.

Who can join?

Anyone who designs anything: UX and UI, graphic, service, industrial, students, career switchers and the design-curious.

Do I need a portfolio or a design job?

No. Come as you are. Half-finished ideas are welcome, and so is just listening.

Does it cost anything?

Meetups are free. If a workshop ever needs a small fee for materials, we say so up front.

Are events in English or Finnish?

Mostly English, so everyone can follow along. Finnish is always welcome over coffee afterwards.

How do I hear about the next event?

Follow us on Instagram or join the mailing list. We post each event at least two weeks ahead.

Can I give a talk or run a workshop?

Yes, please. Tell us your idea at a meetup or send us a message, and we'll help you shape it.
