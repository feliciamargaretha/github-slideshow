About us

Tampere Designs is a community for designers, creatives and IT people in Tampere. We gather, share ideas and make things together, help each other grow, and in turn help the city around us grow too.

How it started

No big plan, just a few designers who wanted somewhere to talk shop outside work and school. It grew from there.

  1. 1a coffee table

    A handful of designers

    A few of us met up to share work and swap tips. Then we kept doing it.

  2. 2word got around

    Friends brought friends

    Students, juniors, seniors and the design-curious joined from every corner of design and tech.

  3. 3today

    A community for the city

    Weekly meetups, guest talks and workshops, and projects that give back to Tampere.

What we care about

  • Everyone's welcome

    No portfolio needed. If you shape how things work, look or feel, you belong here.

  • Learn by doing

    We try things together, share what went wrong, and get better side by side.

  • Give back

    Good design makes a city better. We put our skills to work for Tampere.
