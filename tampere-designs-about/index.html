About us

Tampere Designs is a community for designers, creatives and IT people in Tampere. We gather, share ideas and make things together, help each other grow, and in turn help the city around us grow too.

It started with a handful of designers who wanted somewhere to talk shop outside of work and school.

Today we're students, juniors, seniors and the design-curious, from every corner of design and tech.

We meet to learn from each other, try new things, and give back to the city we live in.
